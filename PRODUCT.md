# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Fintech hiring reviewers: engineers, product managers and recruiters at spend-management companies
(Ramp and its neighbors) who open a portfolio link, often between other tasks, and decide within a few
minutes whether the author can build and reason about spend controls. Confirmed by the author.

## Product Purpose

Card Spend Audit replays two years of Oklahoma's real, public purchase-card transactions through card
controls that decide at the moment of the swipe, checks the same history with after-the-fact rules, and
has an AI reviewer work the largest flags. Every claim is measured against misuse planted in the data on
purpose. Success means the reviewer finishes believing the author can ship a real data product end to
end: honest measurement, working AI, a usable tool. Earning interviews is the goal.

## Positioning

Most spend-control demos assert that controls work. This one measures the trade-off on real public
data: how much planted misuse a swipe-time control stops against how much legitimate spend it declines,
replayed chronologically with no look-ahead, with an AI reviewer tested against planted cases and clean
controls it was not told about.

## Operating Context

- The reviewer arrives from a link (resume, message, GitHub README) and may never install anything.
- Three surfaces: Findings (the argument, with an interactive strictness dial), Case review (80 cases the
  AI worked, with dismiss/escalate), Method (how every number was produced).
- The underlying artifacts are a state purchase-card program: card transactions, merchant names and
  category codes, a state purchase-card policy with a $5,000 single-purchase limit, split-purchase and
  prohibited-category rules, and audit-style case review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

From the project's existing spec and rules (spec.md, CLAUDE.md), not new decisions:
- Static site (React + Vite + Tailwind), hosted on GitHub Pages; the browser only displays JSON the
  Python pipeline writes. No metric logic in the browser.
- Findings are "worth reviewing", never accusations. Cardholder names never appear; cards are "Card A/B".
- Every number on the site is generated by the pipeline; precision on real data is unknown and is never
  implied.
- Light and dark themes; works from 375px wide.
- Charts are hand-written SVG.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real figures from `web/public/data/*.json` (843,229 transactions, $366.5M, 119 agencies; replay curve;
  recall over 5 seeds; 80 AI cases with verdicts, costs and explanations).
- WRITEUP.md and REPORT.md (generated).
- No testimonials, users, customers or endorsements exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the trade-off, not just the win: every control is priced in legitimate spend declined.
2. Measured beats asserted: a claim appears only with the number that backs it and its limits.
3. A reviewer should reach the core result in seconds and the evidence in minutes.
4. Plain language over jargon; terms are explained where they first appear.
